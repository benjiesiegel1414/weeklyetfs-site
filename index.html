Weekly Dividend ETFs: Common Questions

Are there weekly dividend stocks?

Very few individual stocks pay a dividend every week. Most companies pay quarterly and some pay monthly. If you want income every week, weekly dividend ETFs are the main option: they pay out every week, often using covered call or other options strategies. Our list above shows every weekly payer we track, ranked by yield. For the full breakdown, see our weekly dividend stocks guide.

What are the best weekly dividend stocks?

Since almost no stocks pay weekly, most investors use weekly ETFs built on the stocks they already know, like $NVDA, $TSLA and $GOOGL. Our weekly dividend stocks list ranks the top 10 highest paying and best performing weekly payers, with what $10,000 pays you every week.
